PROPERTY MANAGEMENT SYSTEM USER GUIDE

TENANT REGISTRATION

To register a new tenant:

1. Click the Tenants menu.
2. Click Add Tenant.
3. Enter the tenant's personal details.
4. Assign the tenant to a property.
5. Click Save.

RENT PAYMENTS

To record rent:

1. Open Rent Payments.
2. Search for the tenant.
3. Enter the amount paid.
4. Choose the payment date.
5. Save the payment.

REPORTS

To generate rent reports:

1. Open Reports.
2. Select Rent Collection.
3. Choose the month.
4. Click Generate Report.
